Pill-shaped action button; lime `primary` is the default, `green`/`purple` carry nutrition/treino context, `dark`/`outline`/`ghost` are quiet.

```jsx
<Button variant="primary" uppercase icon={<Check size={14} strokeWidth={3} />}>Confirmar</Button>
<Button variant="green" size="sm">Novo Cliente</Button>
<Button variant="outline" size="sm">Substituir</Button>
```

Variants: `primary` (lime, black text), `green` (emerald), `purple` (treino), `dark` (#111), `outline`, `ghost`. Sizes `sm | md | lg`. Press scales to 0.98. Pass `block` for full-width, `uppercase` for CTA tracking.
