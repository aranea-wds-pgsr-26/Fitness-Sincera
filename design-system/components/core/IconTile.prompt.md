Rounded-square icon container - the brand logo tile, meal-block glyphs, total tiles, nav icons. Pass a Lucide icon as the child.

```jsx
<IconTile tone="lime" size={38} glow><Zap size={22} fill="#000" /></IconTile>
<IconTile tone="slate"><Coffee size={18} /></IconTile>
```

Tones: `lime, green, purple, dark, slate, white`. `glow` adds the tone's coloured shadow. Tune `size` and `radius`.
