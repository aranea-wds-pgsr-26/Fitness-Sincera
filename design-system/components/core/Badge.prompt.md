Fully-rounded pill for status, category, or macro labels.

```jsx
<Badge tone="green" dot>Plano Ativo</Badge>
<Badge tone="amber">Em risco</Badge>
<Badge tone="protein">P 32g</Badge>
<Badge tone="lime" solid>Concluído</Badge>
```

Tones: `neutral, lime, green, purple, amber, rose` + macro presets `protein, carbs, fat`. `solid` fills it; `dot` adds a leading status dot.
